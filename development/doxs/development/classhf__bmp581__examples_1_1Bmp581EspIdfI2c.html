class hf_bmp581_examples::Bmp581EspIdfI2c< kPort, kSdaGpio, kSclGpio, kAddr7, kSpeedHz, kTimeoutMs >

Template I2C bridge: one concrete type per (port, SDA, SCL, address, speed).

Call Install() once before constructing bmp581::Driver<Bmp581EspIdfI2c<...>>. Address 0x46 (SDO low) or 0x47 (SDO high). Up to 1 MHz Fm+.
