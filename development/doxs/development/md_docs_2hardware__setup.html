Hardware setup

Electrical

  • VDD 1.71–3.6 V, VDDIO 1.08–3.6 V (independent).
  • I2C up to 1 MHz (Fm+); SPI 4-wire / 3-wire up to 12 MHz; I3C.
  • 2.0 × 2.0 × 0.75 mm metal-lid LGA — use a breakout for bench work.

Address / interface select

Pin I2C SPI
CSB high chip select (active low)
SDO low → 0x46, high → 0x47 MISO

Breakouts: SparkFun SEN-20170 (Qwiic, default 0x47, jumper for 0x46 and SPI), Adafruit 6407 (STEMMA QT, I2C or SPI).

Placement for a barometric reference

  • Vent the sensor to the volume whose pressure you want (enclosure ambient for an atmospheric reference); keep it away from fans and heat sources.
  • Temperature-induced offset is ±0.5 Pa/K — thermal stability matters more than sampling rate.
  • Solder drift is a few tenths of hPa; a one-point comparison against a reference after assembly is good practice when absolute accuracy matters.
